Around the time when people
Bend to fit into the new 
Culture, the one that makes you
Do what everyone
Else says. There are false
Friends that make you feel like broken
Glass. Tears pour from your
Heart. It hurts. You wonder 
If anyone could stop to
Just listen to your words.
Keeping up is a battle to be
Liked by your peers, that
Mock your perfectly
Normal culture, so that
Others could then
Play a selfish game. Their
Quest is to make you forget what
Respect for others actually means.
Support yourself and take some
Time. Rest for a moment and
Unravel your own mind.
Value your culture, and you
Will win, because you stayed yourself.
X-rays will show everyone that
You have a pure heart, while others become 
Zombies
