If you know what you are doing,
you will see it as an investment. 
You can fill the missing wall, 
or add a finishing layer. 
Add proper furniture and this place feels 
complete. A designer would know 
what fits well to make it shine.
Trusting the process can make it 
work, even if you’re no architect.
However, there are risks:
A burglar or an intruder 
could steal all that you built 
for their own gain.
