I woke up in the morning, ate an egg, and headed straight to my class. I am studying to become a tech inventor. In today’s class, the teacher assigned us in groups to do some research, but I asked to work alone. I quietly exhaled when he agreed. After a couple more classes, it was lunchtime, but today the cafeteria was closed because of too many complaints about mice. I had heard some rumors for the past few days but didn’t know that it would cause the cafeteria to close. Since I couldn’t buy lunch, I was left with some free time that I used to fix a watch. Soon I only had one more class left – Electric.

"Class. I noticed my microcircuits were missing. They are quite expensive, so if you find who stole them and give them back, I will thank you. And it’s the end of the class; you are free to go home now,” finally said the professor.

As usual, I went home right away, without paying attention to any of my classmates. But soon a mouse ran by, and everyone near it screamed and ran away in fear. My mother always said I was brave for a girl, since I never understood why most people are so scared of a little creature. It’s not like it could punch you in the face. Wait! There were a bunch of microcircuits lying around the mouse. I bent over to pick them up but saw another one in the mouse's mouth. I picked up what was lying on the ground and started to chase the mouse. As I was running, the ground under me cracked, and I ended up in some hole before I could react. 

After sweeping some broken branches away, I noticed an entrance, but much bigger than the raccoon's den, so it must have been someone bigger. But what are they doing under the school's grounds? I saw a light deeper in the hole, so I climbed inside to investigate. It was narrow at first but became easier the farther I went in, to the point when I could almost stand fully. A strong iron door was in front of me. It was open, so I entered. Inside, there was an eagle?!

But eagles don't live underground like raccoons do. Then I looked to the left and saw an Iceten. The Icetens were creatures that had almost gone extinct a couple of decades ago because of the Creatures’ War. He looked like a log, but under the crust he was dark blue. Then, all of a sudden, the door behind me closed shut, and I was trapped in a basement-looking den. It was dirty and smelled like cedar trees, rust, and damp soil. The shelves were covered in dust. The room was tiny, but I could still stand upright.

"Don't be so scared, Veronica. You wouldn't have followed the mouse if you weren't brave. Sit down on this chair and hear out a deal I want to make with you. I am looking for a partner for my plans, and you are perfect for this role!”

“How do you know my name and why do you want me as your partner?" I asked.

"Since I came to the school's basement, I have been wanting someone who would help me accomplish my plan." said the Iceten creature, eager to tell me his story.
"I speak for him," said an eagle right below the roof.

"I found a mouse for my eyes and ears," said the creature as soon as I saw a mouse in front of me, leaving behind the last circuit. 
"I used the mice to search for the perfect partner. Brave, thoughtful, and craving to know what others do not. And most importantly, someone who wouldn’t run and tell all her classmates. I watched you for a long time. We would work great together."

"You spied on me??? So what do you want from me?"

"Your role is to get past the guards with my eagle. I will give you all the passcodes needed. In return I can get you your own mice so you would be able to know anyone's secret. It’s not so hard,” the creature said, "I will give you some time to think."

The doors behind me opened, and after taking the microcircuit, I headed outside. 

"By the way, my name is Miceten. Nice to meet you."

The next day, I went back to school. I looked around uncomfortably, expecting to see mice, and thought of ways to deal with the mice. At the end of my Forces class, I repeated a phrase in my head three times before approaching my professor and asking in a hoarse voice:
“What sound frequency do mice hate?” During the break, I gathered the materials I needed, and soon I was making a frequency generator. After classes, I went home, finished crafting it, and tested it on low volumes.

Before bed, I was thinking about Miceten's deal. Getting my own mice would be helpful, as I can use them to get more information for tests and even gain access to top-secret science for my own research. But going through guards seems risky. What if I get arrested and it affects my marks? Or what if the eagle is planning to steal or sabotage the government? He didn’t even say where we are going, or maybe he is hiding this information on purpose. I doubt he has any right to even live under the university's basement. To defeat his plans, I would definitely need some help from my professors. I don’t like talking, but it’s essential for my own and school’s safety. 

The next day, I took my frequency speaker and turned it on a bit louder. As soon as I gained enough confidence, I returned the microcircuits, and as I was about to ask, he responded, “Oh, you found my circuits! Thank you so much! If you want, I can help you with a project you have in mind.”

“Oh, yeah, umm. Can I ask you for some help? I met a creature, and I believe he isn’t living there legally or has any good intentions. I was wondering if you could help me trap him before he could escape.”

“Of course, tell me your plan and I will help you as I can. However, you might need to ask others for help as well.” He replied. I sighed but there was no other way – I don’t have time to make everything myself.

I headed home, and as I sat down, I saw a note on my desk. It said "Waiting for your decision. See me today, after school." I am not ready to meet him again, but I need to come so we can get some time to prepare before attacking. I can't take the frequency generator for the mice now or it will make it obvious I am going against him. But I will take my tools to build one just in case.

I entered his basement, once again. "Hey, I haven't decided yet, I would like some more time before my final decision."

"Alright, you can have some time, but first would you do me a favor? Please play a game with me." Miceten replied, taking the dusty board game from the shelf.

"Oh… Sure, unless there are spells or magic in there, I will skip them!”

"There are no spells, but you can skip whatever you want. I just want to play a game with someone who can think."

He took out a Mithril-Vein game; I calmed myself down, and we started playing. The game didn’t have any spells; it was a regular board game. He looked really excited to finally play this game. I guess he just wanted someone other than mice and eagles. Who knows how long he was here without proper communication. But anyway, that will make him trust me and buy us some time.

But in the middle of the game, my phone rang. It was my mom. "Sorry, Miceten, but I need to pick that up.”
"Veronica, quick, go to Maline; your little brother got in a horse accident. We need your help; be quick!" She spoke rapidly because of the adrenaline.

"Open the door; I have to go!" I demanded Miceten.
“Please, just a little bit more. We are almost done. Actually… I just won't open the door until you finish playing with me!" Finishing the game would take too much time, but this is urgent and I need to go now. How can I open this door? Oh wait, I have my toolbox, there should be a tool to help me lockpick this door. 

As soon as I started picking the lock, the eagle attacked me. Hundreds of mice poured out of all the small holes in the den, biting my shirt and skin. With my trembling hands, I still managed to open the door and called the closest taxi. Luckily it was nearby, and I jumped on the horse taxi and directed it to my village, Maline. Some mice were still up on me, trying to bite my hands, but I threw them off me and continued my ride. As I got there, the emergency cabin arrived, and my little brother was taken to the hospital. My mother explained how it happened, and when doctors came back, they were saying that amputation of his leg was necessary.

"Veronica, please, I know you think he is always annoying to you, but without you he won’t be able to stand up anymore! You can craft him a leg or something. Just imagine what it will be like without a leg to support you.” My Mom was asking. As much as he is annoying, he is still my brother, and I have no other choice but to help. 
“Yeah, sure. Do you need anything else?” I asked hoping that’s all. 
“Well… I am sorry I didn’t tell you earlier, but I was fired recently, and I don’t really have enough for your brother… I am sorry.” My mom replied. I sighed but agreed to help. But when I looked at my bank account I realized that I won’t be able to graduate, since I won’t have enough money for my last school year. With my own mice I could earn money faster. But remembering the locked door, I knew I could never trust Miceten.

Soon my brother's condition stabilized. Exhausted, I went back to Edmarine City to think about today. Why was Miceten so eager for me to finish that game? Was it just because of loneliness, or could it be another reason? I decided to do some research about Iceten creatures. Turns out the longer they are with another creature, the easier it is for them to hypnotize someone and fully command whatever they want. Because of such inherited power most people didn’t like them, and in the Creatures’ War they almost ended up extinct. Luckily, my mother’s call saved my life. I have to be quick to finish with all the equipment and get ready.

"Hey, Miceten."
"Oh, hey you came back! Sorry for last time I really wanted to know who would win the game, please forgive me, and be my partner." he begged.
"Okay, fine, I want the mice. But first I want you to see some of my equipment made for you. This one is a dynamic light; just hang it on your ceiling." I told him, handing him the magnetic disk to signal construction workers where to find the exact location of the basement.

As soon as we hung that up, I said, "Hey I also got some sweets for the eagle." As soon as the eagle came close, I caught it, trapping it in the chain.
"Hey! What are you doing?" he shouted. And hundreds of mice ran toward me, but I turned on the speaker, so the sound frequency stopped the mice from coming closer. Soon after, the ground started shaking. I stood next to the iron door as the ground above me collapsed.

They explained that after the war, they became even more aggressive and sought revenge.
